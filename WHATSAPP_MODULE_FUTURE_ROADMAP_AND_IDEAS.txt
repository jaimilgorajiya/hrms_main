2. MANAGER & LEADERSHIP CAPABILITIES (DIRECTLY VIA WHATSAPP)
================================================================================

2.1. One-Click Approval / Rejection via WhatsApp Reply
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Concept:
  When an employee applies for leave or regularization, the reporting manager
  receives a notification on WhatsApp. Currently, managers must log in to the
  portal. With this feature, managers can approve directly by replying on WhatsApp.

Supported Commands for Managers:
  • "approve 101" — Instantly approves request #101
  • "reject 101 Client release pending" — Rejects request #101 with remark
  • Or via Interactive WhatsApp Buttons: [Approve] [Reject]

Flow:
  1. System checks sender's phone to verify they are the designated Reporting Manager.
  2. Updates database status to "Approved" / "Rejected".
  3. Automatically updates employee's Attendance / Leave balance.
  4. Automatically notifies the employee on WhatsApp: "Your request was approved by [Manager Name]".

2.2. Daily Team Attendance Morning Digest (Manager Dashboard)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Concept:
  Every working day at 10:00 AM IST, automatically send each Reporting Manager
  a clean summary of their team's attendance status.

Sample Message:
  Daily Team Attendance — 19 Sep 2026
  Team: Web Development (8 members)

  Present (6):
  • Jaimil Gorajiya (09:15 am)
  • Darshan Rami (09:28 am)
  • Allegra Rice (09:30 am)
  • D Soni (09:32 am)
  • Rohan Patel (09:40 am)
  • Priya Sharma (09:45 am)

  On Leave (1):
  • Vikram Joshi (Casual Leave)

  Not Yet Arrived (1):
  • Neha Gupta (Shift started at 09:30 am)

2.3. Overtime (OT) Request & Manager Approval
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Concept:
  Employees working past shift hours can submit an Overtime claim on WhatsApp:
  • Command: "overtime 2 hours Project release deployment"
  • Sends notification to manager for instant approval.
  • Approved OT automatically feeds into the monthly Payroll calculation.

3. EMPLOYEE SELF-SERVICE ENHANCEMENTS
================================================================================

3.1. Company Holiday Calendar & Upcoming Long Weekends
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Concept:
  Employees can view upcoming company holidays and plan vacations.

Supported Commands:
  • "holidays" — Shows upcoming holidays for the year
  • "next holiday" — Shows immediate next holiday and days remaining
  • "long weekends" — Suggests smart leave dates around holidays

Sample Response:
  Upcoming Company Holidays — 2026

  1. Gandhi Jayanti — 02-10-2026 (Friday)
  2. Diwali / Deepavali — 08-11-2026 (Sunday)
  3. New Year Day — 01-01-2027 (Friday)

  Tip: Taking 1 day leave on Monday gives you a 4-day long weekend.

3.2. Company Policies & HR Knowledge Base Lookup
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Concept:
  Instant search for company HR policies directly on WhatsApp.

Supported Commands:
  • "policy leave" — Shows leave entitlement rules, probation limits, carry-forward
  • "policy probation" — Shows probation duration, evaluation criteria
  • "policy expense" — Shows meal, travel, and mobile reimbursement allowances
  • "policy dress code" — Shows office dress code standards

3.3. Expense Claim & Reimbursement Submission with Receipt Upload
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Concept:
  Employees submit travel, client lunch, or mobile bill reimbursements by sending
  a photo of the bill/receipt directly on WhatsApp.

Flow:
  1. Employee types: "claim expense" or "reimbursement".
  2. Selects Category: 1. Travel, 2. Meals, 3. Mobile, 4. Office Supplies.
  3. Inputs Amount: e.g. "850".
  4. Bot prompts: "Please attach a photo of your receipt/bill".
  5. Employee sends photo -> Backend stores in `/uploads/expenses` and creates
     an Expense Claim for HR/Manager approval.

3.4. HR Helpdesk & Support Tickets
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Concept:
  Employees can raise internal HR or IT tickets without writing emails.

Supported Commands:
  • "ticket ID card damaged"
  • "ticket PF account transfer issue"
  • "ticket monitor not working"
  • Generates ticket number and alerts Admin / IT department.

3.5. Automated Birthday & Work Anniversary Broadcasts
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Concept:
  • At 09:00 AM daily, cron scans for employee birthdays and work anniversaries.
  • Sends personal congratulations message to the celebrant.
  • Optionally sends a morning greeting to the team WhatsApp group.

4. GEOLOCATION, FIELD WORK & SECURITY
================================================================================

4.1. WhatsApp Live Location Punching (Geo-Fencing for Remote/Field Staff)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Concept:
  For field executives (sales, site engineers) or remote employees:
  • When employee types "punch in", bot prompts: "Please share your current location".
  • Employee taps WhatsApp Attachment -> Location -> Share Live/Current Location.
  • Backend checks latitude/longitude against allowed client sites or office radius.
  • Stores exact GPS coordinates and reverse-geocoded address in Attendance punches.

4.2. Selfie Punch Verification via WhatsApp Photo
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Concept:
  If employee setting `requireSelfie === true`:
  • After typing "punch in", bot prompts: "Please send a quick selfie to confirm attendance".
  • Employee sends photo -> Backend validates image and attaches to attendance record.

4.3. Two-Factor Authentication (WhatsApp OTP) for Sensitive Actions
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Concept:
  When logging into the Web Portal from a new device or changing bank account details:
  • System sends a 6-digit OTP to the employee's WhatsApp chat.
  • Higher security than SMS, zero SMS gateway costs.

5. PAYROLL, LOANS & TAX DOCUMENTS
================================================================================

5.1. Form 16 & Tax Deduction (TDS) Certificate Delivery
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Concept:
  Employees can download their annual tax documents:
  • Command: "form 16 2025-26" or "tax slip".
  • Backend verifies employment status and pushes signed PDF document directly.

5.2. Advance Salary & Emergency Loan Application
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Concept:
  Employees in need of emergency funds can apply via WhatsApp:
  • Command: "apply advance" -> Prompts Amount and Repayment Months.
  • Auto-routes to HR/Finance for approval with salary cap validation.

5.3. Increment & Appraisal Letter Delivery
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Concept:
  During annual performance review cycles:
  • Automated batch dispatch of revised compensation letters in PDF to each employee.

6. HR ANNOUNCEMENTS, ONBOARDING & ENGAGEMENT
================================================================================

6.1. Mass HR Announcements & Emergency Broadcasts
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Concept:
  HR Admin dashboard gets a "Send WhatsApp Broadcast" tool:
  • Target: All Employees, Specific Branch, Specific Department, or Specific Shift.
  • Examples:
    - "Office closed tomorrow due to heavy rain / public holiday."
    - "Town Hall meeting today at 4:00 PM in Conference Room A."
    - "Please complete your mandatory POSH training by Friday."

6.2. Automated New Joinee Onboarding Sequence
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Concept:
  When HR adds a new employee with a future joining date:
  • Day -1: "Welcome to [Company Name]! Here is what to expect on your first day."
  • Day 1 (09:00 AM): Office location map, reporting manager details, and Wi-Fi credentials.
  • Day 1 (05:00 PM): "How was your first day? Type 'help' to learn how to punch attendance."
  • Day 7: Check-in survey to evaluate onboarding experience.

6.3. Employee Pulse Surveys & eNPS Polls
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Concept:
  Quarterly 1-question pulse surveys sent via WhatsApp:
  "How satisfied are you with company culture this month? Reply 1 to 5."
  Aggregates anonymous satisfaction scores in Admin HR Dashboard.

7. WHATSAPP INTERACTIVE UI (META INTERACTIVE MESSAGES)
================================================================================

7.1. WhatsApp Interactive Quick Reply Buttons
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Concept:
  Instead of asking employees to type numbers ("1", "2", "yes"), render native
  clickable buttons directly inside WhatsApp.

Button Examples:
  • Main Menu: [Punch In] [Punch Out] [Leave Balance]
  • Confirmation: [Confirm & Submit] [Cancel]
  • Manager Alert: [Approve Leave] [Reject Leave]
  • Duration: [Full Day] [First Half] [Second Half]

7.2. WhatsApp Interactive List Messages (Dropdown Menus)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Concept:
  For selecting Leave Types or Months, open a native pop-up list in WhatsApp:
  • Header: "Select Leave Type"
  • Options:
    - Casual Leave (Remaining: 4 days)
    - Sick Leave (Remaining: 2 days)
    - Privilege Leave (Remaining: 10 days)

7.3. Meta WhatsApp Flows (In-App Modals)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Concept:
  Meta Flows allow complete multi-field forms inside WhatsApp without multi-step chat:
  • Employee taps "Apply Leave" -> A native form sheet opens inside WhatsApp.
  • Selects leave type, date picker for from/to dates, radio buttons for duration,
    and text box for reason -> Taps "Submit".

8. AI-POWERED CONVERSATIONAL INTELLIGENCE (LLM INTEGRATION)
================================================================================

8.1. Freeform Natural Language Understanding (Powered by Gemini / LLM)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Concept:
  Allow employees to speak/type naturally without adhering to exact commands:
  • "Hey I have severe fever today and won't be able to make it to office tomorrow and day after, please apply medical leave"
  • AI parses:
    - Intent: APPLY_LEAVE
    - Type: Sick Leave
    - Dates: Tomorrow to Day After Tomorrow
    - Reason: Severe fever
  • Replies: "I have prepared a 2-day Sick Leave request for 20-09-2026 to 21-09-2026. Shall I submit it?"

8.2. Multilingual HR Assistant
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Concept:
  Support local Indian languages automatically based on employee preference:
  • Hindi: "नमस्ते! आज की उपस्थिति दर्ज करने के लिए 'पंच इन' लिखें।"
  • Gujarati: "નમસ્તે! તમારી હાજરી નોંધવા માટે 'પંચ ઇન' લખો."
  • English: Default corporate English.
